Coverage

Where we post bonds.

We work these facilities every week and know how each one handles bonding and release. If someone is held somewhere not listed here, call anyway. We can usually help or point you to someone who can.

Caddo Correctional Center

1101 Forum Dr, Shreveport, LA 71107
Main line: (318) 677-5254

The main parish jail, run by the Caddo Parish Sheriff’s Office. Most Shreveport and Caddo Parish arrests end up here. Bonding is accepted around the clock. Release times vary with booking volume; evenings and weekends are busiest.

Shreveport City Jail

755 Hope St, Shreveport, LA 71101
Jail desk: (318) 673-7130

Holds people arrested by Shreveport Police on city charges and warrants. A short walk from our office. Bonding is done at the jail itself.

Bossier Parish jails

Bossier Parish Sheriff’s Office facilities, including the Bossier Parish Maximum Security Facility in Plain Dealing

Across the river in Bossier Parish. We post bonds here regularly and know the schedule for transfers between Bossier City and the parish facility.

Bossier City Jail

Bossier City Police Department

For arrests made by Bossier City Police. Many people are moved to the parish facility if a bond isn’t posted promptly, so an early call helps.

Surrounding parishes

We also write bonds in Webster, DeSoto, Red River, and Bienville Parishes, and can arrange transfer bonds for people held outside Northwest Louisiana. Call to check availability for a specific facility.
