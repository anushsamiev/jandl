J&L Bail Bonds (“we”) respects the privacy of the people who contact us. This page explains what we collect through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, phone number, email, and message you enter. Our hosting provider may log standard technical information such as IP address and browser type. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

We use what you send us only to respond to your request and, where a bond is written, to carry out that agreement. We do not sell or rent your information.

Bond records

Information gathered to write a bail bond is handled under Louisiana law and the requirements of our surety. It is shared only with the court, the jail, and the surety as needed to post and maintain the bond.

Third-party services

This site embeds a Google map, which is subject to Google’s privacy policy, and loads fonts from Google Fonts.
