Services

Whatever the charge, we write the bond.

General bail bonds for Caddo and Bossier Parish, from small bench warrants to large felony bonds that need collateral. Here’s what we handle and how each one works.

Misdemeanor bonds

Shoplifting, simple battery, disturbing the peace, criminal trespass, and most traffic offenses. Bonds are usually modest and set by schedule, so release is quick. Often no collateral is needed, only a co-signer.

Felony bonds

Larger bonds that may require collateral such as a vehicle title or property. We explain exactly what’s required up front, work with multiple co-signers when needed, and move quickly once a judge sets bail.

DWI / DUI bonds

First-offense DWI bonds are typically posted within hours. Repeat offenses carry higher amounts and sometimes conditions such as an ignition interlock. We’ll walk you through what the court expects before release.

Domestic violence bonds

In Louisiana these charges usually require a judge to set bail, and release often comes with a no-contact order. We handle these bonds discreetly and help the family understand the conditions so nothing is violated by accident.

Drug charge bonds

Possession through possession with intent and distribution. Bond amounts vary widely by charge and history. Call with the booking details and we’ll tell you where things stand.

Probation and parole holds

Not every hold is bondable, and we’ll tell you honestly when one isn’t. When it is, we know which office to call to get the hold lifted so a bond can be posted.

Warrant walk-throughs

Clear a warrant without spending the night in jail.

If you know there’s a warrant out for you, or you missed a court date and expect one, call us first. We confirm the warrant and bond amount, prepare the bond in advance, and arrange a time for you to turn yourself in. In most cases you’re booked and released the same day.

It’s far better than being pulled over on a Friday evening and waiting until Monday to see a judge.

What we’ll need

Your full name and date of birth, the parish that issued the warrant if you know it, and a co-signer who can sign the paperwork.

Everything you tell us stays between us and the court. We don’t discuss bonds with anyone who isn’t on the paperwork.

Paying for a bond

Options when the premium is more than you have on hand.

The premium is fixed by law at 12%, but how you pay it can be flexible.

Payment plans

For qualified co-signers, we can split the premium into a down payment and scheduled installments. We put the terms in writing before you sign.

Payment methods

Ask which payment methods we accept.

Collateral

For larger bonds, a vehicle title, real estate, or cash deposit can secure the bond. Collateral is returned when the case closes and all court dates have been kept.

Not sure which one applies?

Call with the name and date of birth. We’ll check the jail and tell you exactly what’s needed.
