Questions

The questions we answer every day.

If yours isn’t here, call. There’s no charge for asking.

Cost

How much does a bail bond cost in Louisiana?

The premium is set by state law at 12% of the bond amount, with a minimum of $120. A $5,000 bond costs $600. The court may add a small statutory fee. The premium is not refunded, because it is the fee for guaranteeing the full bond.

Do I get the money back when the case is over?

The premium, no. It is earned once the bond is posted. Collateral, yes: anything pledged to secure the bond is returned when the case closes and all court dates have been kept.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, for qualified co-signers. We take a down payment and set up the balance in installments, with the terms in writing before anything is signed.

Will I need collateral?

Usually not for smaller bonds. On larger bonds we may ask for a vehicle title, real estate, or a cash deposit. We’ll tell you up front, before you commit to anything.

Timing

How long until they’re released?

We can usually have the bond written and delivered within an hour of your call. Release after that depends on the jail. At Caddo Correctional Center it can range from a couple of hours to much longer during busy periods. We give you an honest estimate once we’ve spoken to the jail.

Bond hasn’t been set yet. Can you still help?

Yes. Many Caddo Parish charges have a bond schedule, so an amount may already be available. For others, a judge sets bail at first appearance, typically within 72 hours. We track it and move the moment a bond is set.

Can you bond someone out on a weekend or holiday?

Yes. We’re open 24 hours every day and the jails accept bonds around the clock.

Co-signing

What does a co-signer agree to?

A co-signer (the indemnitor) promises that the defendant will appear at every court date and agrees to be responsible for the full bond amount if they don’t. In practice, that means staying in touch with the defendant and with us. Most co-signers never hear from us again except for court-date reminders.

Who can co-sign?

An adult with a valid photo ID, a stable address, and a source of income. Family members are most common, but friends and employers can co-sign too.

Can I co-sign from out of town?

Yes. Call us and we’ll explain how paperwork and payment work for out-of-town co-signers.

After release

What happens if a court date is missed?

The court can forfeit the bond and issue a warrant. Call us immediately if a date is missed or about to be. In many cases we can help get the defendant back in front of the judge before a forfeiture becomes final.

Can the defendant leave the state?

Not without checking first. Travel restrictions depend on the court’s conditions. Ask us before any trip and we’ll tell you what the bond allows.

What if the charges are dropped?

The bond is released and any collateral is returned. The premium is not refunded, since the bond did its job during the time it was in force.

Is bail the same as a fine?

No. Bail is a guarantee of appearance, not a penalty. Fines are decided only if there’s a conviction, and they’re separate from anything paid for the bond.
