About us

Local people, on the other end of the line.

J&L Bail Bonds is a small, licensed bail office on Texas Street in downtown Shreveport. We’ve built our name on answering the phone and telling people the truth.

How we work

Most people who call us have never dealt with a jail before. They’re scared, it’s late, and they’ve been given a phone number and a dollar amount they don’t understand. Our job is to turn that into a plan in the first five minutes of the call.

That means explaining the premium before you ask, telling you honestly how long release is likely to take, and never pressuring anyone into a bond they can’t afford. If a bond can’t be posted, or there’s a hold the jail won’t lift, we say so.

We’re licensed in Louisiana, and every premium we charge is the one the state sets.

What you can expect

Four promises we keep on every bond.

We answer

Not a service, not a voicemail. An agent who can start your bond on the spot, at 3 p.m. or 3 a.m.

We quote the law

The premium is 12% of the bond, set by Louisiana statute. We’ll show you the math and put it in writing before you pay anything.

We stay with it

From the first call through release and every court date. We send reminders and we pick up when you have a question months later.

We keep it private

Who we bond out, and who paid for it, is nobody’s business but yours and the court’s.

Find us downtown

Our office is at 400 Texas Street, Suite 570, in downtown Shreveport. We’re open 24 hours, so call ahead and we’ll tell you the best way in.

You don’t have to come in to start a bond. Call first and we’ll tell you what’s needed and where to sign.
